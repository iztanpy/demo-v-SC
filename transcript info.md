Call Summary

Escalation: The incident was escalated when elevated radial vibration persisted despite normal bearing health indicators, suggesting a structural fault mechanism with potential loss-of-containment risk.

Troubleshooting: The team correlated vibration signatures with casing inspection data, performed a targeted review of the discharge-side volute weld region, and identified a surface-breaking discontinuity approximately 60 mm from the weld toe consistent with fatigue initiation.

Revised Diagnosis Rationale: The diagnosis was revised to pump casing fatigue cracking after ruling out bearing spalling and matching the crack location, morphology, and vibration response to a previously documented cyclic pressure-load failure mode on a comparable BFP asset.

Recommendation: Shutdown of gas turbine. 4h, 50MW, $2.4M revenue at risk. Emergency Maintenance and Trading desk notified.
